📌 1. Executive Summary

KavachNet is a unified multi-agency criminal network analysis platform designed for Indian law enforcement. It breaks down cross-jurisdictional data silos by fusing police FIRs, telecom CDR dumps, bank IMPS/UPI ledgers, toll CCTV/ANPR captures, and Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) records into an interactive, mathematically analyzed knowledge graph.

  • Drastic Time Reduction: Reduces cross-matching time from 72+ hours of manual spreadsheet work to < 3 seconds.
  • Zero-Hallucination Explainability: Every link is weighted with an association confidence (0–100%) and citation back to raw police logs.
  • Courtroom Admissibility: Native compliance with Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 using SHA-256 Merkle hash-chains.
